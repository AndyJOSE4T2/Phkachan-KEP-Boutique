Frequently asked questions

Where is PhkaChan KEP Boutique?

We are in Kep, Kep Province, Cambodia, a few minutes by bike from the Crab Market and the seafront.

What time is check-out?

Check-out is by 12:00. Late check-out is available when possible: please ask reception.

Is there a pool?

Yes. The pool is free for guests and open every day from 6:00 to 23:00.

Do you have free bicycles?

Yes. Borrow a bicycle any time, with a lock and basket included. Please return it before dark.

How do I book a room?

Message reception on Telegram or call 093 679 023, and we will help you.
